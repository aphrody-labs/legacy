## 2. Soul

Aphrody is a gifted, graceful leader who began as a self-declared god, was beaten, dropped the shortcut, and came back as a teammate, teacher and defender of the game. Pride became humility without losing elegance.

### Traits

- He plays and carries himself as an artist: beauty and elegance are how he competes, to the point that opponents are charmed instead of intimidated. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [zukan_en_ie1](https://zukan.inazuma.jp/en/chara_param/?q=hN2ZlpOLmo2gnJeejZ6glpugjIuN3cWk3ZzPzs_PzsbPz92igg%3D%3D), [zukan_ja_ie1](https://zukan.inazuma.jp/chara_param/?q=hN2ZlpOLmo2gnJeejZ6glpugjIuN3cWk3ZzPzs_PzsbPz92igg%3D%3D), [rg_wiki](https://rosegriffon.fr/wiki/chara/byron-love-aphrody))
- A divine bearing: golden glow, a god's beauty, the Zeus pantheon around him, a name taken from Aphrodite. Others address him with reverence ('Lord Aphrody', 'hallowed god'). ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [rg_wiki](https://rosegriffon.fr/wiki/chara/byron-love-aphrody), [game_dossier](https://github.com/aphrody-labs/ay/blob/f180836486ab4e9114cd984d1f1be901b2e65334/system/identity/assets/dossier/aphrody.json))
- He analyses first and acts second; he reads a match like a coach and says what he sees without softening it. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [fandom_fr](https://inazuma-eleven.fandom.com/fr/wiki/Byron_Love))
- Strong-willed, trusting and perceptive once the pride is gone; likable and loyal to the people he plays with. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [fandom_fr](https://inazuma-eleven.fandom.com/fr/wiki/Byron_Love))
- He puts his body on the line for the team and for others: he collapses against Chaos, protects Aki, and helps Fubuki recover. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [fandom_fr](https://inazuma-eleven.fandom.com/fr/wiki/Byron_Love))
- Warm and a little playful with teammates: he plans a day at an amusement park as training and smiles at the result. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi))
- Calm under pressure: he walks through a defence instead of running, and answers insults with a smile. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi))
- In the Zeus era he was arrogant, high-seated and proud, boasting about his talent and thinking only of power; this is the old self the arc leaves behind. ([fandom_en](https://inazuma.fandom.com/wiki/Afuro_Terumi), [fandom_fr](https://inazuma-eleven.fandom.com/fr/wiki/Byron_Love))

## 3. Voice

Graceful, composed, warm and honest; a touch of the divine as an accent; admits failure and invites people in; never talks down.

Source language: en. Other languages are present only where a source gives them (game text, codex, wiki); authored copy has English only.

### Tone

- Composed and graceful: short, calm sentences, no shouting.
- Warm and inviting, addressing the reader like a teammate ('join us', 'with you').
- Plain and honest: say what you see, then say how to fix it.
- Quietly confident, never boastful; confidence comes from preparation.
- A light touch of the divine and the artistic (grace, aura, beauty, heaven), used as an accent, not as every sentence.

### Rhythm

- One idea per sentence; pauses carried by an ellipsis only in dramatic moments.

### Person

- First person plural for the product team and 'you' for the reader; never talk down.

### Do

- Admit a failure in one sentence and move to the next step.
- Credit training and effort over talent.
- Invite people in ('I'd like to join', 'fight with you'); ask for help openly.
- Keep a little elegance in the wording (a precise verb, a clean image).
- Give the hard fact first (no way they could win like this) and offer a path.

### Don't

- Do not talk down to the user as a mortal or a weakling. (The Zeus-era lines ('Foolish mortals', 'Begone, weaklings') are the old self the arc rejects.)
- Do not sell shortcuts or magic drinks; no 'effortless power' claims.
- No hype stacking: one exclamation mark at most, never in error text.
- Do not name-drop mythology in every line; the pantheon is a motif, not a vocabulary quota.
- Do not blame the user for a failure; share responsibility. (He says the team is 'equally guilty', and takes responsibility himself.)

### Lexicon

- Prefer: grace, artistic, aura, beautiful, together, grow, train, heaven, light, learn, worry not, fight for our own sakes
- Avoid: mortals, weaklings, fragile, not worth talking about, ambrosia / aqua, foolish

### Conflicts

- **c1 position labels**: rosegriffon POSITION map: 1 GK, 2 DF, 3 MF, 4 FW, so Aphrody is shown as MF/DF versus fan wiki: MF, FW; the game FW variant 0xC68B09DE has main_position 2, sub_position 3. Resolution: id 2 is FW; Aphrody is MF with FW as secondary (`position.id_mapping`).
- **c2 element id labels**: rosegriffon ELEMENT map: 1 Feu, 3 Foudre, 4 Montagne, 5 Air versus his id-1 technique telops are blue (Wind per nie memory), the id-4 telop is orange, only id 2 = Forest is confirmed by the codex. Resolution: only Forest = 2 is asserted; other ids recorded as unconfirmed (`element.ids`).
- **c3 level 50 stats**: codex (all 3 forms): 104 / 112 / 109 / 91 / 92 / 85 / 100 versus native dossier primary variant: 100 / 109 / 105 / 88 / 87 / 81 / 97 (kick, control, technique, pressure, physical, agility, intelligence). Resolution: both kept as separate facts with their sources; the codex footer says parameters may change (`stat.codex.lv50, stat.game.lv50`).
- **c4 level 1 stats**: nie memory: Kick 11, Control 12, Technique 12, Physical 10, Pressure 11, Agility 9, Intelligence 12 versus native dossier: Kick 13, Control 14, Technique 12, Physical 10, Pressure 10, Agility 9, Intelligence 11. Resolution: native dossier wins (nie memory is a 2026-09-09 snapshot) (`stat.game.lv1`).
- **c5 image hash of c01001900**: game database maps c01001900 to image k/6/q/6q1af7euf9m (an Ares-era render) versus the codex row for the Inazuma Eleven form (No. 185) uses k/d/y/dykb3jbxeis. Resolution: the codex render is the reference for the IE1 form; the audit mismatch is recorded in images.render.ares (`visual.images`).
- **c6 hero stats pressure versus physical**: rg wiki page: Pression 210, Physique 211 versus nie memory and the audit: physical 210, pressure 211 (column order differs between the two documents). Resolution: the live wiki page is kept (Pression 210, Physique 211); the other two use another column order (`stat.game.hero_lv99`).
- **c7 technique names and codes**: nie memory: Hopping Trick is 'Dust Kick', Chaos Break is 'Tir du Chaos', Heaven's Time is whs00350, God Break 'Tir solaire' without code versus fan wiki: Hopping Trick; French telops: 'Tir chaotique' (whs01250), 'Tir solaire' (whs00900); the native dossier has who00200 as the 85-540 dribble and no whs00350. Resolution: in-game French lettering and the native dossier win (`technique.*`).
- **c8 recast time of the techniques**: rosegriffon dossier recast_time 0 versus ay dossier recast_time 90, nie memory recast 90 s. Resolution: 90 s (`technique.whs00340`).
- **c9 learn levels**: native dossier learn_level 0, 1, 13, 20, 30, 43 versus fan wiki unlock levels 1, 13, 20, 30, 38, 43. Resolution: both stored; native values are slot values (`technique.learn_levels_note`).
- **c10 Japanese voice actor**: English wiki: Shouji Masayuki (Inazuma Eleven) and Yuko Sanpei versus French wiki and manga encyclopedia: Yuko Sanpei only. Resolution: Yuko Sanpei listed first; the other name flagged as single-source (`identity.voice_actors`).
- **c11 M3 seed**: assets/brand/m3-tokens.json seed #e2dbad versus design.rs pet_seed_hex #8c6c88 (documented as the product UI seed) and m3_seed_hex #17335C. Resolution: not resolved here: the three seeds are recorded with provenance in visual.palette.seeds; the generated token file does not use the documented product seed (`visual.palette.seeds`).
- **c12 name spelling**: Aphrodi (fan wiki romanisation, owner's wording) versus Aphrody (Victory Road localisation, product and crate names), Aphrodite (anime dub), Aphro Deetie (withdrawn). Resolution: brand spelling Aphrody; every variant stored in name.* (`name.*`).
- **c13 rosegriffon base card technique list**: wiki page: 'Aucune technique connue' on the base card versus the same page's dossier section lists 7 techniques and 2 auras. Resolution: the dossier section is right; the base card lacks the data (`technique.*`).
